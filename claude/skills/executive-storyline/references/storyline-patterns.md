# Storyline patterns

Pick the archetype from the audience's starting point and the decision. Slide counts are for a
5–12 slide core deck; extra material goes to the appendix.

## SCR / SCQA (situation → complication → [question] → resolution)
- **When** the audience does not yet feel the problem, or the recommendation only makes sense
  once the complication is accepted.
- **Skeleton** 1 governing thought (answer first, even in SCR) · 1–2 situation (shared facts) ·
  2–3 complication (what changed / what breaks, with evidence) · 2–3 resolution · 1 ask.
- **Pitfall** a long "situation" that re-tells what the audience knows. Keep it to one slide.

## Answer-first + pillars (inductive)
- **When** the audience expects a recommendation and needs the reasons.
- **Skeleton** 1 recommendation · 1 exec summary (pillars as sentences) · 1–2 slides per pillar ·
  1 risks & mitigations · 1 next steps/ask.
- **Pitfall** pillars that overlap (not MECE) or that are activities, not reasons.

## Diagnosis → root cause → prescription
- **When** something underperforms and the audience wants to know why and what to do.
- **Skeleton** symptom (the number) · where it happens (decomposition) · why (root cause, causal
  chain) · what to change (prescription mapped to causes) · how we'll know (metrics).
- **Pitfall** jumping from symptom to solution without the causal link.

## Options → criteria → recommendation
- **When** a choice among alternatives must be made and defended.
- **Skeleton** the decision to make · options (MECE, including "do nothing") · criteria and
  weights · evaluation · recommendation + what would change our mind.
- **Pitfall** straw-man options; criteria chosen after the answer.

## From–to transformation
- **When** the story is a shift in operating model, strategy or behaviour.
- **Skeleton** why change now · from → to shifts (3–6) · what the "to" looks like (model) ·
  roadmap · what leadership must do.
- **Pitfall** "to" states that are slogans; no mechanism.

## Value at stake
- **When** sizing an opportunity or a loss drives urgency.
- **Skeleton** size of the prize (hero metric) · where it sits (decomposition) · why we are not
  capturing it (leakage) · what captures it · the first 90 days.

## Status + decision (steering committee)
- **When** a recurring forum needs to know if things are on track and what to decide.
- **Skeleton** overall status in one sentence · what moved (bridge vs plan) · risks that need a
  decision · the decisions requested (explicit options).
- **Pitfall** activity reporting ("we held 12 workshops").

## Role vocabulary (for `role_in_story`)
`hook` · `governing_thought` · `frame` · `evidence` · `diagnosis` · `root_cause` · `pivot` ·
`resolution` · `recommendation` · `how` · `proof` · `risk` · `ask` · `breather` · `appendix`.
