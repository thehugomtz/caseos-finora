# Synthesis examples

## Topic titles → action titles

| Topic (✗) | Conclusion (✓) |
|---|---|
| Resultados Q2 | Q2 creció 8%, pero todo vino de precio: el volumen cayó 3% |
| Análisis de mercado | El mercado crece 12% anual, pero solo el segmento premium es rentable |
| Situación actual de IA | Tenemos 42 pilotos de IA y solo 6 generan valor medible |
| Funnel de ventas | Perdemos 70% de los leads calificados en la primera llamada |
| Próximos pasos | Necesitamos tres decisiones del comité antes del 15 de noviembre |
| Benchmark de competidores | Somos líderes en precio pero últimos en tiempo de respuesta |
| Customer journey | El cliente decide en la visita, no en el anuncio: ahí debemos invertir |

Rule of thumb: if the title could be the heading of a table of contents, it is a topic.

## Lists → relationships

**"Métricas: CAC, LTV, churn, ARPU, ARR"** — five KPI boxes say nothing. Ask what the audience
must conclude. Likely relationships:
- *Unit economics*: `LTV = ARPU × margen bruto ÷ churn`; health = `LTV / CAC`; payback =
  `CAC ÷ (ARPU × margen)`. Message: "El ratio LTV/CAC cae de 4.1 a 2.3 porque el churn se duplicó,
  no porque CAC subiera" → driver tree with the churn branch as focal.
- *Growth accounting*: `ARR final = inicial + nuevo + expansión − churn − contracción` → bridge.
- *Efficiency frontier*: channels on CAC × LTV → scatter with the frontier.
If none is clear, **ask the user** which decision the metrics inform.

**"Hallazgos: 1…7"** — cluster into 2–4 themes, find the one that explains the others (root
cause), make it the message; the rest become support or appendix.

**"Pilares estratégicos: A, B, C, D"** — are they stages (sequence), layers (dependency), levers
(drivers of one outcome) or bets (portfolio positions)? The answer defines the story *and* the
visual.

**"Riesgos: 1…8"** — plot on likelihood × impact; the message is the 2 that need a decision now.

## Data dump → insight

Input: a table of 12 regions × 6 metrics. Wrong: the table. Right: find the pattern that matters
for the decision ("3 regiones concentran 70% de la caída y comparten el mismo canal") → a ranked
contribution with those 3 highlighted; the table goes to the appendix.

## Process description → finding

Input: "Hicimos 25 entrevistas, 3 talleres y analizamos 2 años de datos". This is method, not
message. It goes to a footnote or the appendix. The slide states what we found.

## Red flags in a storyline

- Two slides in a row whose titles could be swapped (no logical order).
- A title with "y" joining two unrelated claims.
- A recommendation slide before any evidence slide that supports it.
- A slide whose message is only a number without a comparison.
- A title that the slide's content does not prove.
- More than one slide of "context" before the first insight.
